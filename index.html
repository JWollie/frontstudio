04 / Good to know

No small print
energy.

A few straight answers before we start.

How quickly can I get online?

For a one-page site, we aim to show you a first draft within three business days of receiving your short brief and 50% deposit. That's a draft target, not a guaranteed launch date. We confirm availability and timing in your quote. Feedback, approvals, any missing assets and the final payment determine launch. Larger sites have a separate, agreed timeline.

Do I need to write the copy?

No finished copy needed. Both website packages include a first draft for your included pages, based on a short brief about your business, customers and services. We refine it with you, and you check facts and approve the words before launch. Specialist research, long-form articles or content beyond the agreed pages are separate scopes, quoted upfront.

What do you need from me?

The basics: what you do, who you help, your contact details and what you want customers to do next. Share your logo, photos and genuine reviews if you have them. If something is missing, we'll agree how to handle it before starting. You don't need a polished brief.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the final invoice is paid, the website design, code and content are yours. Your domain stays in your name. There's no compulsory care plan or lock-in, and we hand everything over if you'd like to move.

What if I want changes later?

Unlimited small tweaks are included during the build and for your first 30 days after launch. After that, small edits are NZ$75 each, or unlimited on the optional NZ$149/month care plan. Small means under 15 minutes, like updating photos, prices, hours or wording. Larger changes, such as a new page or feature, get a fixed quote before we start.

Is the care plan compulsory?

No. Without care, we set up hosting in your own account and hand over the site. Your domain and any hosting charges are paid by you. Care adds hosting, uptime monitoring, unlimited small edits, form and domain renewal checks, and a reply within two business days. You can cancel any time.

Are the demos included in the packages?

The Lab shows possibilities, not bundled features or real client work. Estimators, booking systems, online shops and other custom interactions are quoted separately. Your package already includes a mobile-friendly site, first-draft copy, enquiry form, click-to-call and basic search setup. We agree what you need before any work starts.

Will I be first on Google?

No one can promise that. We set up sensible page titles, descriptions and Google Business Profile links so search engines can understand your site. Ongoing SEO is separate, and rankings depend on factors beyond the website itself.
